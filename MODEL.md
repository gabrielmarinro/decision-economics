# Model

The model is designed around a connected economic state rather than isolated financial outputs.

## Core dimensions

- Customers and fleet size
- Pricing and revenue
- Google Cloud and shared infrastructure costs
- Telcel and other operating costs
- Fixed and variable OpEx
- Team capacity and operating leverage
- Break-even and payback
- Burn rate and runway

The source system models an 18-month horizon and exposes client-level and consolidated economics. It calculates revenue, cost, margin, break-even and related measures from the active model state.

## Decision outputs

The model supports:

**What-if analysis → sensitivity → scenario comparison → risk exposure → decision**

The objective is to make economic consequences visible before a business decision is committed.
